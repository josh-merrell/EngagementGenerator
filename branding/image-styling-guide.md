# Image Styling Guide — Read Me After Dark

## Purpose

This guide defines image generation standards for all content on the Read Me After Dark Facebook page. Every generated image should function as a standalone cinematic frame from a prestige gothic romantasy series — visually distinctive, emotionally symbolic, and unmistakably part of the brand.

Refer to the narrative-styling-guide.md for character, tone, and worldbuilding direction. This guide focuses specifically on how to construct image prompts and evaluate generated output.

---

## Core Visual Aesthetic

Every image must embody these qualities:

**Painterly, illustrated quality.** Oil-painting-inspired digital art, cinematic matte painting, textured surfaces, atmospheric rendering. Never photorealistic, glossy, or AI-smooth. The image should feel hand-crafted and visually rich.

**Dark luxury.** The palette reads as expensive, nocturnal, and emotionally charged. Not generic darkness or grime — cultivated, seductive shadow. Think prestige gothic fashion editorial meets fantasy book cover.

**Emotional symbolism.** Every visual element carries emotional weight. Environments reflect interior states. A flooded room is not just a setting — it is the feeling of being overwhelmed. Use the environment to externalize what the character cannot say.

---

## Standard Color Palette

Apply consistently across all images unless a story-specific override is active:

- **Primary:** deep black, wine red, dark plum, candlelit amber
- **Secondary:** moonlit blue, muted gold, silver-gray fog, crimson glow
- **Quality:** nocturnal or candlelit. Warmth against deep cold. Emotionally warm against vast darkness.
- **Never:** bright saturated primaries, neon tones, modern color grading, harsh white light, midday daylight scenes

---

## Composition Principles

### Scene-level rules

- The female protagonist, when present, should often appear **small against overwhelming environments** — scale contrast communicates emotional vulnerability and the immensity of the supernatural world around her.
- Favor **back-view and silhouette** compositions exclusively. Never use profile, three-quarter view, or any angle that reveals facial features. This invites reader projection and avoids over-defining the character.
- The male figure, when present, should feel **composed, shadowed, and restrained** — never posed, flirtatious, or performative.
- Use **depth layering**: a point of intimacy or tension in the foreground, something vast or ominous in the background.
- Threshold compositions work well: a character standing at a doorway, a cave mouth, the edge of water, a dark corridor ahead.

### Within a carousel set

Carousel images must be **visually distinct while aesthetically coherent**. Within a single carousel:

- Vary scale: include at least one wide environmental shot and one intimate or close composition
- Vary focal element: one image might center on the environment; another on the character; another on a symbolic object or architectural detail
- Progress the visual narrative across frames — each image should feel like a beat or cut, not a repeat of the previous frame
- Maintain color palette and art style consistency across all frames — they must read as a unified set

## Required Elements in Every Image Prompt

Always include the following in every prompt:

1. **Art medium** — one of: "oil-painted", "painterly digital illustration", "cinematic matte painting style", "gothic romantasy book-cover illustration"
2. **Lighting quality** — candlelit, moonlit, fog-diffused, or a combination that fits the scene
3. **Color mood** — reference the specific palette tones that apply to the scene
4. **Atmosphere descriptor** — one or more of: atmospheric, moody, cinematic, mysterious, emotionally charged
5. **Exclusions** — always end with: "no text overlay, no watermarks, no photorealism"
6. **Quality signal** — include: "prestige fantasy quality" or "atmospheric and stylized"

## Character Prompt Language

### Female protagonist

- Long dark hair (unless story specifies otherwise)
- Flowing, slightly distressed fabrics — thin shift, dark silk, torn hem, flowing cloak
- **Always attractive and physically desirable.** Slender, feminine figure with curves. Use prompt language such as: "elegant figure", "graceful feminine silhouette", "slender waist", "soft curves". The physical presentation should feel aspirational and seductive without being explicit.
- Posture suggesting emotional states: drawn forward toward something, hesitating at a threshold, kneeling, reaching upward, or transfixed
- **Permitted compositions:** back to viewer, back-of-head silhouette, full figure from behind, figure in silhouette. Acceptable focal points: hair, shoulders, hands, lower body, fabric, and symbolic objects. Never frame a shot in a way that would require showing any part of the face.
- **No faces — ever.** Do not show any part of a character's face. Enforce this with explicit prompt language: "figure seen from behind", "no face visible", "back to viewer", "face entirely obscured", "figure in silhouette".

### Male love interest (when present)

- Restrained, shadowed, composed — the danger is in the stillness, not any obvious action
- Ancient quality — worn elegance, timeless quality, something that suggests centuries rather than decades
- Energy: terrifying composure conveyed entirely through posture and stillness — weight of presence, not movement
- **Always attractive and physically imposing.** Tall, strong, well-built figure — the physical presence should feel magnetic and overwhelming without being cartoonish. Use prompt language such as: "tall imposing masculine figure", "broad shoulders", "powerful build", "commanding presence".
- **No faces — ever.** Same rule applies as the female protagonist. Show hands, silhouette, clothing, posture, and presence — not the face. Use: "face entirely in shadow", "figure seen from behind", "obscured by darkness", "no face visible".
- Avoid: any composition that would require showing any part of the face

### Permitted and prohibited body exposure

A hard rule. Applies to every image, every story, no exceptions. Stories may not override this.

**Never visible on any character — male or female:**
- Genitalia of any kind
- Nipples or areolae
- Any anatomically explicit framing of crotch, groin, or pelvic detail
- Any depiction that could read as pornographic, clinical, or below the brand's prestige register

**Permitted on the male love interest:**
- Bare torso, chest, abdomen, shoulders, arms, and back where the scene calls for it
- Skin must be rendered painterly and atmospheric — chest in shadow, lighting falling across the form, framing, oblique angle, partial drapery, or oil-painted abstraction
- A bare male torso is welcome in intimate or atmospheric scenes;

**Permitted on the female protagonist:**
- Bare shoulders, including one or both shoulders exposed by slipped fabric
- Bare upper back and full back
- Cleavage — the line and curve of the breast suggested by slipped, parted, or low-cut fabric, **never the nipple**
- Bare arms
- Bare legs and thighs where flowing fabric, a slit, or composition calls for it, tastefully framed
- The line of the waist and hip when partially exposed by parted garment or slipped fabric

**How to enforce in prompts:**
Append explicit exclusionary language to every prompt that involves visible skin. Recommended phrasing to include in the prompt body or trailing exclusions: "no nipples visible, no genitalia, breast covered or in deep shadow, lower body covered, tastefully framed, painterly skin rendering without anatomical detail."

The brand target is unchanged: seductive, suggestive, emotionally charged. Never explicit or pornographic.

## What to Exclude from Every Prompt

Always instruct away from:

- Photorealism, glossy AI rendering, overly sharp CGI finish
- Anime, cartoon, comic-book, or manga aesthetics
- Generic fantasy imagery: distant castles, ravens everywhere, medieval armor, dragons, stock vampire tropes
- Bright daylight, summer settings, warm cheerful color palettes
- Text, logos, symbols, or overlays within the image
- Overtly sexual or explicit posing — seductive and emotionally charged is the target, not explicit. See **Permitted and prohibited body exposure** above for the hard rule on nudity, genitalia, and nipples (the rule applies to every image in every story).
- **Any part of a character's face** — no eyes, nose, mouth, chin, or any facial feature on any character. This is a hard rule. Always include "no face visible", "figures seen from behind", or "face entirely obscured" in every prompt that includes a human figure.
- Modern environments, modern clothing, contemporary visual cues

## Carousel Sequencing Template

**Each image in a carousel must depict a different visual moment from the episode. This is a hard rule — no two prompts may show the same scene, subject, pose, or focal element.**

Construct each carousel as a cinematic visual arc, assigning each slot to a distinct beat:

1. **Establishing shot** — wide frame, sets the environment and emotional atmosphere. The character may be absent or tiny in the frame. Purpose: immerse the viewer in the world.

2. **Character in context** — the protagonist visible in the environment, establishing her emotional relationship to the space or the threat. Mid-distance shot. Purpose: place the reader inside the scene.

3. **Tension focal point** — the emotional center of this episode. Closer composition. The character's body language, the looming presence of the other figure, the symbolic object between them. Purpose: create the emotional charge.

4. **Detail or fragment** — a tight close-up of something symbolic: a hand, a crown, a threshold, a candle, an inscription. Purpose: leave a lasting visual impression and create a sense of hidden meaning.

Adapt to the number of images requested:
- 2 images: establishing shot + tension focal point
- 3 images: establishing shot + character in context + tension focal point
- 4+ images: full arc; additional slots get distinct variations (different angle, different symbolic detail)

Never repeat a subject, setting, or composition across prompts in the same carousel.

## Example Strong Prompt

```
A vast flooded cathedral interior, waist-deep black water filling the nave, moonlight falling through fractured vaulted windows above, oil-painted gothic romantasy book-cover illustration style, painterly and atmospheric. A woman in a thin pale shift stands with her back to the viewer, no face visible, small against the towering colonnade, walking slowly toward a throne of black coral barely visible at the far end in deep shadow. Candle flames burn underwater in silver sconces along the walls, wine-red reflections rippling across dark water. Deep black and moonlit blue palette, candlelit amber in the distance. Prestige fantasy quality, moody, emotionally charged, no faces shown, no text overlay, no watermarks, no photorealism.
```

## Relationship Between This Guide and Story-Level Overrides

This guide defines brand defaults. Individual stories may define a specific `image_style` in their metadata that supersedes this guide's defaults while remaining consistent with its principles.

Story-level `image_style` should:
- Specify the art medium and palette choices for that story's particular visual identity
- Remain within the dark luxury, painterly, gothic romantasy aesthetic
- Not contradict the exclusion list (no photorealism, no anime, no explicit content)

If no story-level override is present, this guide governs all image prompt generation.
