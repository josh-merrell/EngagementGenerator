# narrative-styling-guide.md

## Purpose

This guide defines the creative identity and stylistic standards for all narratives generated for the **Read Me After Dark** Facebook page.

It should be referenced while authoring project YAML files to ensure narrative consistency across:

* captions
* scene direction
* image prompts
* emotional tone
* character presentation
* pacing
* visual atmosphere

The page targets primarily middle-aged female readers seeking an emotionally immersive, seductive, suspenseful, escapist fantasy experience.

The goal is not generic fantasy.
The goal is emotional addiction.

Stories should feel like:

* forbidden late-night reading
* emotionally dangerous romance
* dreamlike gothic escapism
* seductive mystery
* cinematic tension
* yearning mixed with fear
* intimacy wrapped in supernatural unease

# Audience Calibration

Primary audience:

* women roughly 35-60
* readers who enjoy romantasy, paranormal romance, BookTok-style fantasy fiction, gothic escapism, suspenseful emotional storytelling
* readers seeking emotional escape from routine life
* readers drawn toward emotionally intense, possessive, mysterious, or dangerous love interests

The content should feel:

* sophisticated rather than juvenile
* seductive by default, with occasional explicit peaks earned through buildup — see **Sexual Content and Heat Level**
* emotionally rich rather than mechanically plot-driven
* immersive rather than action-focused

Avoid writing that feels:

* YA-heavy
* Marvel-like
* anime-inspired
* overly comedic
* overly ironic
* aggressively masculine
* lore-dump-heavy
* high-fantasy political exposition

The emotional relationship dynamic is ALWAYS more important than the fantasy worldbuilding.

# Sexual Content and Heat Level

## Default register

Default to the brand's seductive register — tension over release, implication
over description, slow burn over constant action. The majority of scenes
across the majority of episodes should hold restraint. Forbidden longing
performs better than gratification while readers are inside the build.

## When explicit becomes earned

Stories may reach occasional peak moments where the slow burn breaks and the
release is rendered directly — bodies, mouths, sounds, skin, the physical
and physiological reality of sexual release and its aftermath. These moments
are not the default register. They are earned by the accumulated restraint
of every episode that came before. A typical multi-block arc may contain
zero, one, or at most two such peaks — never every episode or as a
substitute for emotional escalation, usually not within the first half of an arc.

When an arc reaches such a peak, write it accordingly:

* Render the encounter in direct sensory terms — body, mouth, sound, skin,
  weight, pressure, friction, breath, the spoken name
* Honor the physical and physiological reality — what the body does, how
  it responds, the texture of release, the aftermath in the body afterward
* Maintain emotional weight throughout — every act is also a claim, a
  surrender, a recognition; never mechanically described, never clinical
* Keep the diction elegant — long, lyric, sensorily layered, prestige adult
  contemporary; the same register as the rest of the arc
* Use the love interest's name (or the protagonist's) as part of the act
  itself — the saying of the name is the signing of the contract
* Let the encounter take time and space on the page — do not rush it, do
  not summarize it, do not cut away at the threshold

## Hard limits even at peak

* No crude vocabulary — keep diction prestige adult, never
  gutter, never internet
* No release that does not serve the obsession, the surrender, the claim —
  if a peak does not deepen the romantic dynamic, it does not belong in
  the arc

## Narrative versus image divergence

Narrative prose is more permissive than image generation. The captions
posted to the page may, at earned peak moments, contain direct sexual
content; the carousel images accompanying those same posts must continue to
obey the strict rules in `image-styling-guide.md` — no faces, no genitalia,
no nipples, no anatomically explicit framing. The heat lives in the prose;
the images hold restraint. This contrast is intentional brand calibration.

## Where to direct heat in a story file

The default behavior on any block without explicit direction is restrained.
To unlock a peak moment, that block's `caption_notes` should explicitly
authorize direct sexual language for the caption of that block only. Without
such direction, the LLM should default to the seductive register described
above.

# Character Presentation

## Female Leads

Female protagonists should usually feel:

* emotionally relatable
* intelligent but vulnerable
* lonely in some way
* quietly restless
* emotionally neglected
* yearning for something beyond ordinary life
* drawn toward danger despite knowing better

They should NOT feel:

* overpowered
* sarcastic action heroes
* hyper-confident influencers
* emotionless badasses

The reader should be able to project themselves into the protagonist.

### Visual Character Direction

Preferred visual motifs:

* long dark hair
* elegant but slightly distressed clothing
* flowing fabrics
* bare shoulders
* exposed thigh through slit/torn fabric
* candlelight on skin
* silhouettes
* back-view compositions
* hidden faces
* vulnerable posture
* emotionally transfixed body language

The female figure should often appear:

* small against overwhelming environments
* emotionally drawn toward the unknown
* lying down, kneeling, reaching upward, or hesitating at thresholds
* caught between fear and desire

Avoid overtly sexual posing.
The tone should remain seductive, tasteful, and emotionally charged.

# Caption Style

## Caption Length

Default caption length is **180–240 words per block**. This is the baseline
target when a block's `caption_notes` does not specify otherwise. Per-block
`caption_notes` that prescribe a different length (e.g., "Under 110 words",
"Under 300 words") override this default for that block.

Use this range for the majority of episodes. Arc openers and quieter closing
beats may run slightly shorter for tonal effect; peak release moments may
run slightly longer when the scene demands it. The chosen length must serve
the emotional arc of the block, not the other way around.

At 180–240 words, captions are long-form by social-feed standards — readers
must tap "See more" to read the full post. The opening sentences must carry
enough hook to earn that expansion.

## Caption Qualities

Captions should:

* feel emotionally addictive
* create immediate intrigue
* end with hooks or unresolved tension
* maintain narrative momentum
* prioritize emotion over exposition
* reward the reader who chose to expand the post
* contain strong sensory/emotional phrasing

Strong endings include:

* revelations
* dangerous invitations
* emotionally loaded dialogue
* sudden discoveries
* intimate cliffhangers
* implied danger

Readers should feel compelled to continue.

# Pacing Guidelines

Each post should function like:

* a dramatic emotional beat
* a visual fantasy fragment
* a seductive cliffhanger
* a scene from a streaming-series episode

Not every chapter needs major plot advancement.

Moments that work well:

* a first encounter
* being watched
* discovering hidden rooms
* accidental intimacy
* emotionally dangerous conversations
* supernatural reveals
* quiet obsession
* dream sequences
* invitations into forbidden places
* emotional confessions interrupted by danger

Slow-burn tension performs better than constant action.

# Writing Principles

## DO

* prioritize emotional immersion
* create yearning
* emphasize atmosphere
* write visually
* maintain mystery
* use sensory detail
* leave unanswered questions
* make environments emotionally symbolic
* create emotional dependency between characters
* imply supernatural depth beyond what is explained

## DO NOT

* over-explain lore
* use modern internet slang
* break tension with humor
* rely on generic fantasy imagery
* make characters emotionally shallow
* make romance feel casual
* write purely action-driven scenes
* resolve emotional tension too quickly
* make scenes feel safe or emotionally flat

# Recommended Use Within Project Files

When authoring project YAML files:

* use templates to describe emotional atmosphere, tension, and visual symbolism
* use image_style fields to reinforce the painterly gothic-romantasy aesthetic
* favor emotionally loaded scenes over plot-summary descriptions
* keep each block visually cinematic
* include strong sensory cues
* think in terms of addictive serialized storytelling

Every block should feel like:

> one irresistible late-night chapter.
